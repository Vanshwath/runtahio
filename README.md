## 📁 About the application

Computers gather files over time. These files take up space. You might find your drive full and not know why. Runtahio helps you see what takes up space on your disks. It creates a map of your files. This map shows you which folders and files are large. You can then choose what to keep and what to remove. 

This tool uses a sunburst chart and a treemap layout. These visual tools show the size of your folders. A larger block means a larger folder or file. You can navigate through these blocks to drill down into your disk contents. This makes finding hidden clutter easy.

## ⚙️ System requirements

Runtahio works on modern computers. You need a system that supports current disk management tools. Ensure your computer has at least 4 GB of RAM. You should have at least 100 MB of free disk space to install the app. 

The application runs on Windows 10 or Windows 11. Your user account must have administrator rights to scan all folders on your drive. If you do not have these rights, the app might not see some protected system folders.

## 🔍 How to scan your drive

When you open the app, you see a list of your connected drives. Pick the drive you want to scan. Click the scan button. The app reads your file system. It draws a sunburst chart of your storage. Wait for the progress bar to finish.

The chart shows folders as colored segments. The center of the sunburst is the drive root. The outer rings are folders and files. Larger segments represent folders with more content. Click a segment to zoom in on a specific folder. 

## 🗑️ Cleaning up space

Identify the files you no longer need. Select a folder or file to highlight it. You can hover your mouse over any segment to see its name and size. Right-click a segment for options. You can choose to move items to the Recycle Bin. 

Always check the contents of a folder before you remove it. Do not remove files if you do not recognize their names. Many folders contain system files necessary for Windows to run. Runtahio highlights folders that are safe to clean. 

## 🛠️ Frequently asked questions

### Will this app remove my personal files?
The app only removes files if you command it to do so. It does not delete anything on its own. You have full control over what goes to the Recycle Bin.

### How do I refresh the view?
Click the refresh button in the top toolbar to scan your drive again. This updates the chart with your current disk state.

### Can I change the color scheme?
You can choose a color theme in the settings menu. Themes help make the map easier to read for different users. Dark mode is also available for low-light environments.

### What happens if I make a mistake?
If you move a file to the Recycle Bin by mistake, you can restore it. Open your Recycle Bin on your desktop. Find the file, right-click it, and select Restore. This puts the file back in its original position.

### Does the app need an internet connection?
The app works offline. You do not need an internet connection to scan your disks or clear your files. The app only needs a connection to check for updates.

## 💡 Troubleshooting tips

If the app fails to start, restart your computer. This clears stuck processes that might block the app. Ensure you have installed all Windows updates. If you still see errors, uninstall the app through your control panel and run the installer again. 

If the scan stops, your disk might have errors. Run the built-in Windows disk repair tool to scan your drive for errors. A healthy drive allows the app to perform at its best. If you see a permission error, make sure you run the app as an administrator. 

The application is built with modern tools to ensure stability. It handles large file systems without using too much memory. If your computer slows down during a scan, close other demanding applications. Large disks with millions of files take longer to scan. Give the app time to finish the process.

## 📈 Improving performance

Keep your drive organized to maintain speed. Run a scan once a month. Delete large temporary files and old installers. Removing clutter helps your computer work faster. You will notice better response times when your drive has plenty of empty space. Use the sunburst chart to track your progress over time. Seeing your storage usage helps you build better file management habits.
